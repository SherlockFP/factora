All files land in `assets/props/`. Same conventions as the stalls: Blender Z-up, front face −Y,
`export_yup=True` (so three.js gets Y-up with the front toward +Z), flat-shaded, vertex/material
colours only, 1 unit = 1 metre, ground at z = 0.
