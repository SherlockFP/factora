Grow a Garden is a  game created by  and bought by  under the community . In the game, players plant seeds in their garden and sell crops for money. It is one of the biggest games on Roblox.

==Gameplay==
Players begin by buying seeds to grow their garden. Once their crops are finished growing, they can sell them (to Sam) for Sheckles to buy more seeds, or upgrade to better ones. The game also has other shops, such as a Pet Shop and Gear Shop. Random events happen throughout the game, and they have a chance to mutate crops for higher resale value. Some mutations also link to an hourly event, which they can trade with an exclusive currency for newer items.

==Trivia==

* Grow a Garden currently holds multiple records:
*# Most visits gained in a week - 2,052,822,520.
*# First game to hit 5,000,000 concurrent players.
*# First game to hit 10,000,000 concurrent players.
*# First game to hit 15,000,000 concurrent players.
*# First game to hit 20,000,000 concurrent players.
*# First standalone game in gaming history to hit 20,000,000 concurrent players.
*# Has passed Fortnite in active players.
*# Has helped Roblox get the most players online in any video game in a single day.
*# Has the most visits from a game in a non-verified group.
*# The game's CCU record is 22,375,597 players.
*# The game rapidly grew in popularity for its rewarding and accessible gameplay, becoming one of the fastest-growing games on Roblox.
* According to , Grow a Garden was the first game to make an event called Admin Abuse.
* Grow a Garden is actively developed by Do Big Studios™ and Splitting Point Studios.
* After declining between January and March 2026 due to key departures and unpopular updates, the game experienced a major revival in April 2026 with the release of the Easter Event 2026 update.
