Retail Tycoon 2 is a building tycoon experience created by . It is an improved sequel to Retail Tycoon.

==Gameplay==
The experience's main objective is for players to build and manage a retail store while managing their cash wisely. Other mechanics are also vital to a store, such as building and shelf stocking. Each store also has a rating, which are dictated by multiple factors, including shelter,  decorations, crowdedness, and cleanliness.

=== Products ===
Players can sell any of the following products in their store. Each product has a buy price, a sell price, and a unit size. The buy price defines the amount of money the player must spend to purchase one product, whereas the sell price determines the amount of money the player makes when the product is sold. The unit size determines the size in storage of each individual item.

== Sponsorship events ==

Between November 10, 2022, and November 30, 2022, Retail Tycoon 2 partnered with Norelco to promote Movember, a men's health awareness campaign. A Philips OneBlade product was added to the experience as an item that can be sold in players' stores. Those that played the experience during the sponsorship are still able to purchase and sell the Philips OneBlade product after the event.
