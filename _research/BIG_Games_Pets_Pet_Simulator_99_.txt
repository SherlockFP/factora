Pet Simulator 99 is a Roblox experience developed by . It is the fourth game in the Pet Simulator series. It was created in February 2022, and released on December 1, 2023.

==Gameplay==
The core gameplay is similar to the other installments in the franchise, revolving around hatching eggs to get pets, collecting currencies and unlocking more areas and pet eggs. However, pets now break obstacles on their own, and the players can damage obstacles directly by tapping them. When the 'pets follow taps'-setting is on, the pets of the player with this setting on also go to that obstacle.

==Trivia==

* Pet Simulator 99 is the sequel to Pet Simulator X.
* The game currently has over 2000 unique pets to be collected, not including gold, rainbow, or shiny variants.
* Pet Simulator 99 has hundreds of real-life merchandise items, sold globally.
* There are 6 user badges in the game: one is a welcome badge, one is for The Hunt: First Edition, and 4 were for The Hunt: Mega Edition, 2 of which were unobtainable.
* Pet Simulator 99 has participated in 2 official Roblox events.
** In 2024, it participated in The Hunt: First Edition.
** In 2025, it participated in The Hunt: Mega Edition.
