Steal a Brainrot™ is a  game created by  and  under the community  and the corporation Do Big Studios™. Players can purchase, defend, and steal various Brainrot characters. It is also the second biggest game on Roblox.

==Gameplay==
The Player spawns at one of the 8 bases on the map, and the player earns cash from brainrots in their own base. Upon joining a server, the base is locked for 30 seconds. and can be locked again for 60 seconds once the timer expires. Each rebirth increases the lock time by 10 seconds (e.g. 80 seconds for two rebirths, 110 for five rebirths and 240 seconds for last 18 rebirth), but the initial lock time will still be 30 seconds.

There are two ways of obtaining a brainrot. The first one is purchasing one from the red conveyor in the middle of the map, with its price increasing every time another player tries to get it. Another way is stealing a brainrot from another user's unlocked base. However, when a brainrot is stolen, the thief is slowed, stripped of all items, and the owner is alerted, making the stealer vulnerable to attacks from any other user. Attacking a thief will teleport the brainrot back to its original base. Players defend their brainrots from other players by using items and locking their base.

===Tutorial===
The player starts off with 100 cash. The game tells the player to buy a Noobini Pizzanini from the red conveyor and wait for it to reach the player's base. Then the game tells to earn cash from that brainrot, and afterwards, the tutorial tells the player to steal brainrots from other players' bases and bring them to the player's base.

===Base===
The base is the building in which players hold their brainrots and collect money from them. It is a building with a gate consisting of red laser beams. Only the owner (and optionally their Connections) can go through that gate. The second rebirth will add a second floor to the base and an outside staircase on the right side of the base, the 10th rebirth will add another floor with a ladder on the inside of the second floor. The first floor can hold 10 brainrots, the second floor holds 8 and the third floor holds 7 brainrots at their maximum capacity, meaning players can hold up to 27 brainrots in their base (at 18 rebirths). Since the August 1st update, brainrots can be transferred to another slot within the base.

===Rebirth===
The player can rebirth each time if the player has met the requirements; for example, the first rebirth requires 1 million cash, a Trippi Troppi, and a Gangster Footera <small>(formerly Tung Tung Tung Sahur)</small>. Each rebirth increases base lock time by 10 seconds, raises the money multiplier by 1, and unlocks stronger shop items. Starting from the second rebirth, each rebirth will also add 1 brainrot slot to the player's base. Rebirthing will erase all brainrots in the player's base and the money will be reset to a fixed amount. Items are not affected by rebirths. Players can rebirth up to 18 times.

===Admin Abuse===
Admin Abuse is an event hosted on Tuesdays at 6:00pm ET and Saturdays at 3:00pm ET, unless otherwise stated. During an Admin Abuse, various events and lucky blocks spawn along with high-tier brainrots. The events usually last between 30-45 minutes. Hosts will also run luck events, ranging from 4x to 35x server luck multipliers.

==Points of interest==

===Shops===
There are currently three shops in the middle of the red conveyor. The Shop contains items purchasable with cash, and the Robux Shop containing items that cost Robux. The player can also find a spinning wheel next to the Robux shop, with the wheel choosing one of the six prizes that the player can get.

====Items====
Items can be used to increase the player's speed, make them jump higher, or attack other players. The starting item that all players have is the Tung Bat which knocks back players and recovers stolen brainrots. Additional items can be bought in the Coins Shop. Players start off with 3 items in the shop and unlock more items with each rebirth. Purchased items disappear from the inventory upon leaving the experience, so they must be rebought after rejoining. This can also be done by an auto-buy feature which buys the desired items immediately upon joining the game. The Tung Bat (and items from the Blackhole Starter Pack) will always remain in the inventory.

The shop items are listed in the Rebirth section.

===Fuse Machine===
The Fuse Machine was located next to the Coins Shop. To activate it, the player had to give 4 brainrots to the machine. More valuable brainrots in the machine extended the fuse time and the money to fuse the brainrot but gave a more valuable brainrot in return. All of the brainrots that could be fused in the fuse machine except couldn't be spawned on the conveyor or obtained in lucky blocks.

Except the following:

* Tukanno Bananno: glitch where it could spawn on the carpet the first 1-3 days after getting added.
* Brr Es Teh Patipum: get it during admin abuse. (Sammy's Base)
* Los Orcalitos: during the Orcalero ritual.

However, when the Los Lucky Block was added, Los Tungtungtungcitos and Los Combinasionas became obtainable again.

The Fuse Machine was removed on September 6 and was replaced by the Craft Machine.

===Craft Machine===
The Craft Machine replaced the Fuse Machine and was located next to the coin shop. Every 30 minutes, players would have one option of each rarity to craft. After the thirty minutes was over, the options would shuffle, but sometimes during admin abuses, Sammy would skip the time or add certain brainrots to the machine. Players would put 2-4 brainrots to turn them into a better version or a combination version. The rarer the brainrot, the more expensive and timely it was to craft (Epic: 7m, Legendary: 15m, Mythic: 30m, Brainrot God: 45m, Secret: 1h30m).

Many Craft Machine brainrots were nearly unobtainable because they needed either fuse machine brainrots or limited ones. To get them, you would have had to not delete the brainrot, which many players did not do because the brainrots didn't make much money.

The Craft Machine was removed on October 11 and was replaced by the Witch Fuse.

==Brainrots==
There are currently 8 rarities of brainrots: Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Brainrot God, Secret and OG. The exact spawn rates are not available to the public. A brainrot with a higher rarity will be more expensive but also generate more money. Mutations and traits increase the amount of money it will generate but do not increase the price. A Legendary brainrot is guaranteed to spawn every 5 minutes, while a Mythic brainrot spawns every 15 minutes, and a guaranteed Brainrot God spawns whenever a certain number of likes is reached (this is disabled in private servers). Below are all available brainrots, including how much money they generate per second ($/s; without any mutations or traits) and their purchase price. The sell price is half of the purchase price.

The Lucky Block brainrots can spawn normally as well. An asterisk denotes only admins can spawn this brainrot.

===Mutations and Traits===

==== Mutations ====
As of April 2026, brainrots can naturally have one of these 4 mutations:  

* Gold (Multiplier x1.25)
* Diamond (Multiplier x1.5)
* Divine (Multiplier x10)
* Rainbow (Multiplier x10)
Other mutations were in previous versions and are no longer available to get, unless spawned by an admin:
* Bloodrot (Multiplier x2)
* Celestial (Multiplier x4)
* Candy (Multiplier x4)
* Lava (Multiplier x6)
* Galaxy (Multiplier x7)
* Yin Yang (Multiplier x7.5)
* Radioactive (Multiplier x8.5)
* Cursed (Multiplier x9)
Mutations are not stackable. (A brainrot can have only one mutation)

====Traits====
Traits are obtained by brainrots as they go along the conveyor when an event is going on. They are denoted by a symbol that appears along their info above them. Traits are stackable, and mutated brainrots can obtain traits too.

As of September 27, there are 7 events that can be spawned by performing a ritual:

** An asteroid will occasionally strike a brainrot and give it a purple aura.
** Has an asteroid symbol.
* <u>Bombardiro</u> (Multiplier x4): 3 people must grab a Bombardiro Crocodilo and line up at the start of the red conveyor.
** Spawns a Los Crocodillitos.

** Has a Bombardiro Crocodilo symbol.
* <u>Orcalero (Shark Fin)</u> (Multiplier x4): 4 people must grab an Orcalero Orcala and stand next to each other.
** Spawns a Los Orcalitos.
** The map is submerged in water, reducing gravity.

** Has a shark fin symbol.

**Spawns a Los Matteos.

===Index===
The player can view the brainrots and their rarities that they have collected by clicking on the index button. Each primary mutation possesses its own index. The completion of any index adds 0.5× to the money multiplier. The achievement of a mutation's index will also give the player a base in the color of the mutation. For example, a golden base for the golden index, and so on.
